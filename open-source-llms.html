Best open-source LLMs: 12 open models you can run yourself

Open-weight language models can be downloaded and run on your own hardware or server, so your data stays with you. These are 12 from the AI Download Center catalog, from small models for laptops and phones to large ones for servers. Each one links to its maker’s official page.

They are grouped by size and purpose, not ranked or benchmarked. Licences differ between models, and some limit commercial use, so read each model’s licence before you build on it.

How to choose

  • Running on a laptop or phone? Start with Llama 3.2, Phi-4 or Gemma 4.
  • Want one model for most tasks? Try Llama 4, Qwen3 or Mistral Large 3.
  • Mainly reasoning or code? Look at DeepSeek V4 or DeepSeek R1.
  • Need an app to run them? See our guide to local LLM apps such as Ollama and LM Studio.
