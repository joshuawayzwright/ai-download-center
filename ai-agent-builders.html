AI agent builders and automation tools: 10 platforms in one list

Want AI to do work for you, not just chat? These are 10 tools from the AI Download Center catalog for building AI agents and automations: no-code platforms you set up in a browser, open-source builders you can host yourself, and frameworks for developers. Each one links to its official site.

They are grouped by how much code you need, not ranked. Some are cloud services with free plans; the open-source ones are free to self-host, but you pay for your own hosting and any AI model usage.

How to choose

  • No coding at all? Start with Zapier AI or Relevance AI.
  • Want to own the setup and keep data on your server? Look at n8n, Dify or Langflow.
  • A developer building agents in code? Compare LangGraph, CrewAI and AutoGen.
  • Need answers from your own documents? LlamaIndex and Haystack are built for that.
