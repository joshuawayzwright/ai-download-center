# SOLO Digital Store — Commercial Product Reset (27 October 2026)

## Decision
- Retire the **AI Marketing Kit** from new sales and promotion. Owner reports **one month, zero external sales, one self-test purchase**.
- Preserve historical customer access, refunds, payment records and fulfilment files. Do not delete or disable historical transactions.
- No false claims of bestseller status or guaranteed demand.
- Do **not** activate Stripe checkout until the specific product file, preview, licensing, refund wording, paid delivery and post-purchase test pass.

## Market signal
- Australian SMBs report regular AI use for data processing, administration, customer service and bookkeeping (QuickBooks 2026 survey); functional workflow tools match those use cases.
- Search-demand reports point to editable spreadsheets, budgeting templates, Canva templates and niche digital tools rather than generic PDF advice.
- These are **category-level demand indicators**, not proof these particular SOLO products will sell. Validate with landing-page interest and purchases.

## Priorities
1. Build and test **Cashflow Control** and **Quote & Invoice Builder** first. Both offer concrete, editable utility.
2. Validate one customer journey (preview → Stripe → payment → secure download → email → refund/support).
3. Add each other product only when its files are complete and QA passes.
4. Track real external orders, product-page conversion, refunds and support requests; distinguish self-tests.

## Launch gates per product
- [ ] Deliverable created (not just mockup)
- [ ] Calculations and examples independently tested
- [ ] Accurate listing and screenshots
- [ ] File quality, mobile preview, accessibility
- [ ] Correct Stripe price/link and payment metadata
- [ ] Protected fulfilment, no public paid-file URL
- [ ] Test purchase and customer delivery verified
- [ ] Refund/support path checked
