FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear boundaries before work starts.

Can you guarantee more enquiries or revenue?

No. Results depend on traffic quality, demand, pricing, reputation, competition and implementation.

Do you need access to my website?

Not for the audit or copy package. Access is requested only for approved implementation after backups and least-privilege access are agreed.

How fast is delivery?

The audit has a priority target of two business days after payment and completed intake. Timing is confirmed in writing before payment; larger scopes receive a separate schedule.

No. The service is delivered remotely across Australia. Implementation depends on the client's platform and access arrangements.

AI may assist research, drafting and consistency checks. Client-facing work is reviewed, edited and checked by a person before delivery.
