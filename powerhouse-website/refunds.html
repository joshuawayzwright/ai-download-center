Refunds & Consumer Rights

How to get help with a purchase, delivery issue or refund request.

Last updated
27 October 2026
Applies to
Purchases & customer support

This policy covers digital product purchases from The Solo Service Provider, including earlier orders. Our new launch collection is in development and is not available to purchase yet.

Change of mind

Where the product has been correctly supplied and there is no problem with it, a change of mind does not automatically create a right to a refund. We may still consider reasonable requests case by case.

Australian Consumer Law

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not excluded, restricted or modified by this policy. If the product does not meet applicable consumer guarantees, remedies may include a replacement, refund or other remedy depending on the circumstances and the nature of the problem.

Delivery problems

If you paid successfully but did not receive access or delivery instructions, first check spam or junk mail, then contact hello@thesoloserviceprovider.com.au with the email address used at checkout.

Requesting help

Email hello@thesoloserviceprovider.com.au with your order details and a clear description of the issue. We will assess the request against the product supplied, the issue reported and applicable consumer law.
