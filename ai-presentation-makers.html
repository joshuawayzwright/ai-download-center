AI presentation makers: 5 tools that build slides from a prompt

Need a slide deck fast? These 5 AI presentation makers from the AI Download Center catalog turn a prompt or an outline into slides you can edit, share and present. Each one links to its official site.

They are grouped by how you work, not ranked. Pay attention to export: if your school or company uses PowerPoint or Google Slides, check that the plan you pick can export to it.

How to choose

  • Want to try for free? Gamma, Pitch and SlidesGPT have free plans.
  • Must hand in a PowerPoint file? Gamma exports PPTX on its free plan; Pitch and SlidesGPT export on paid plans; Beautiful.ai exports editable PowerPoint.
  • Presenting to a class? Prezi adds live quizzes and polls.
  • Need images for your slides? See our AI image generators guide.
