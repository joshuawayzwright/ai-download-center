Offline AI tools: 12 apps and models that run without the internet

Want AI that keeps your data on your own computer? These are 12 tools from the AI Download Center catalog that run locally once they are set up: chat apps, document assistants, transcription and image generation. Each one links to its official site.

They are grouped by what you want to do. Running AI locally needs a reasonably capable computer, and downloading apps and models needs internet the first time; check each project’s requirements before you start.

How to choose

  • Just want a private ChatGPT-style app? Install Hob, LM Studio, Jan or GPT4All.
  • Need answers from your own documents? Look at AnythingLLM or PrivateGPT.
  • Transcribing interviews or meetings? Whisper runs locally and is open source.
  • Making images without a subscription? Stable Diffusion 3.5 and Flux.1 are open models; they need a capable graphics card.
