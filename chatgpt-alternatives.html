How to choose

  • Want the closest all-rounder to ChatGPT? Try Claude, Gemini or Copilot.
  • Need answers you can fact-check? Use Perplexity or You.com, which show sources.
  • Want to compare models side by side? Poe puts several in one chat app.
  • Want nothing to leave your computer? Use Hob, LM Studio, Jan or GPT4All. They need a reasonably recent computer; check each app’s requirements.
