Best AI image generators: 9 tools, grouped by how you use them

This guide covers nine hand-picked AI image generators from the AI Download Center catalog: Stable Diffusion 3.5, SDXL, Flux.1, GPT Image, Midjourney, Ideogram, Leonardo AI, Playground and Adobe Firefly. Of these nine, three are open models you can run yourself, four are freemium web apps, and two (GPT Image and Midjourney) are paid. Each entry links straight to the maker's official site. The full catalog also lists community model cards from Hugging Face, including specific FLUX.1 and SDXL checkpoints.

This list isn't ranked, and we haven't tested or benchmarked these tools. Instead, it groups them by how people use them: open models you download and run, hosted generators you use in a browser or through an API, and design-focused tools for logos, typography and marketing visuals.

Open models you run yourself

These are model weights, not apps. You download them and run them with your own software and hardware, or through a hosted service that offers them. "Open source" is the catalog's price type. Each model has its own license, so read it before you use the output commercially. If you also want to run language models on your own machine, see our guide to local LLM apps.

Stable Diffusion 3.5

A high-quality text-to-image model from Stability AI for generating photorealistic and artistic visuals.

Who it suits: People who want to generate images on their own hardware and control the whole setup.

License note: The official Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large model page says it's under the Stability Community License, "free for research, non-commercial, and commercial use for organizations or individuals with less than $1M in total annual revenue." Larger organizations need an enterprise license for commercial use.

Open sourceOfficial site

SDXL

Stability AI's XL-scale diffusion model, used for advanced image generation and editing workflows.

Who it suits: People who want a widely used open model to generate and modify images from text prompts.

License note: The official SDXL Base 1.0 model page lists the CreativeML Open RAIL++-M License.

Open sourceOfficial site

Flux.1

A generative model from Black Forest Labs for highly detailed image creation and design experimentation.

Who it suits: People who want open weights for detailed images and are comfortable checking which variant's license fits their use.

License note: Black Forest Labs' official GitHub repository lists FLUX.1 [schnell] under Apache 2.0, and FLUX.1 [dev] and its variants (Fill, Canny, Depth, Redux, Kontext, Krea) under the FLUX.1-dev Non-Commercial License.

Open sourceOfficial repository

Hosted generators

These run on the maker's servers. You type a prompt in a browser or call an API, and there's no model to install.

GPT Image

OpenAI's image generation model, available in ChatGPT and the API.

Who it suits: People who already use ChatGPT, and developers who want to add image generation to an app. OpenAI's API documentation describes both generating images from a text prompt and editing existing images with a new prompt.

PaidOfficial site

Midjourney

An AI image platform known for stylized, cinematic and concept art generation.

Who it suits: Artists and creatives who care most about a distinctive visual style.

PaidOfficial site

Leonardo AI

A generative art platform used for game asset design, concept art and visual experimentation.

Who it suits: Game developers and concept artists who want a web app built around asset and concept work.

FreemiumOfficial site

Design-focused tools

These are aimed at designers and marketers who need finished graphics, not only standalone images.

Ideogram

An image generator focused on branding, typography and polished render outputs.

Who it suits: People making images that need text in them, such as brand graphics and posters.

FreemiumOfficial site

Playground

An AI graphic design tool for logos, social posts and marketing visuals.

Who it suits: Small businesses and creators who want ready-to-use designs. Its homepage lists templates for things like logos, social media posts, t-shirts, stickers and posters, and says it's "Free to start" with "No credit card." It doesn't state the free plan's limits there.

FreemiumOfficial site

Adobe Firefly

Adobe's creative AI suite for image generation, text effects and design workflows.

Who it suits: Designers who already work in Adobe tools, and teams that want to know where the training data came from. Adobe's Firefly page says its Firefly models "are trained on licensed content from Adobe Stock and public domain content where copyright has expired." The same page lists video and audio tools alongside image generation.

Free option: Adobe's page says the free option includes "limited free daily generations across both standard and premium features." It doesn't give an exact number on that page.

FreemiumOfficial site

How to choose

  • Want to run a model on your own hardware? Start with Stable Diffusion 3.5, SDXL or Flux.1, and read each model's license first.
  • Want to generate images in a chat or from your own code? Look at GPT Image.
  • Want a strong visual style for art and concepts? Look at Midjourney or Leonardo AI.
  • Need text, logos or marketing graphics? Look at Ideogram, Playground or Adobe Firefly.
  • Want to try before you pay? The four freemium tools (Leonardo AI, Ideogram, Playground and Adobe Firefly) have a free way to start. Check each official pricing page for current limits, because they change.
