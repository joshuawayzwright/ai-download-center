# Zeely setup: AI Download Center

Values for Zeely's **Brand DNA** screen. Colours come from `styles.css`; voice and rules come from [`../brand.md`](../brand.md). If those files change, update this one too.

## Identity

- **Logo:** [`logo-512.png`](logo-512.png), a 512px PNG export of `favicon.svg`. This is the SOLO "S" emblem, which the directory also uses.
- **Colours:** Primary `#F2C766` · Secondary `#139EE8` · Text `#F2F3F4` · Background `#0A0A0A`
- **Fonts:** Headline: Georgia, or the closest serif Zeely offers. Body: Inter, or any clean sans-serif.
- **Visual style:** Minimalism, Futuristic
- **Visual don'ts:** robots or glowing brains; fake app screenshots; other companies' logos shown as partners; emoji clutter

## Branding

- **Overview:** AI Download Center is a free, searchable directory of AI models, chatbots, assistants, self-hosted apps and creative tools, with links to official download pages. It's for developers, students and teams who want to compare AI tools fast without SEO spam. AI startups can pay for a clearly labelled featured listing to reach those visitors.
- **Values:** Trust, Transparency, Simplicity
- **Tagline:** Find the right AI tool. Skip the spam.

## Communication

- **Tone:** Simplicity, Friendly, Professional
- **Taboo words and claims:** revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, 10x, "#1" or "best" rankings, user or traffic numbers, "official partner of" any AI company, "every tool is hand-picked"
- **Headline styles to avoid:** clickbait questions, ALL CAPS, fake urgency
- **Always avoid:** implying OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or Meta endorse the site; promoting a featured listing without saying it's sponsored; invented reviews or testimonials
