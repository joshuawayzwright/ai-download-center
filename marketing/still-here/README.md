**Overview:** Still Here is a memoir by Joshua Wright. As a child in Redbank Plains he lost his mother to murder. By eighteen he was in prison. It follows rehab, years clean, fatherhood, relapse, and the work of facing the people he hurt. It's for readers who have lived through trauma, addiction or loss, or love someone who has: an honest account of what it takes to keep going.

## Meta rule to remember

Meta's personal-attributes policy bars ads that assert or imply the viewer's health or addiction status ("Struggling with addiction?"). Write about the author's own story, in first person. If an ad is rejected over the word "heroin", change "away from heroin" to "clean".
