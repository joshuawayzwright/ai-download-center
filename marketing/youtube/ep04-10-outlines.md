# Episodes 4–10: outlines

Working outlines, one per video. Each lists the takeaway, the test to run, the structure, and what to verify. Turn one into a full script once its test is done; the format follows episodes 1–3.

Keyword figures are vidIQ estimates from 15 Nov 2026. Re-check demand and competition before you record. Competition is a 0–100 score; lower is easier to rank for.

---

## Episode 4: I Saved [X] Hours a Week With AI: The Exact Workflow

- **Keyword:** save time with ai (about 9K searches a month, competition 23)
- **Takeaway:** a measured before-and-after of one week of your admin, with the exact AI steps that saved the time.
- **The test (2 weeks):** week A, log your admin time with no AI (a spreadsheet: task, minutes). Week B, use the episode 2 workflows. **The title's number must come from this log.** If the saving is small, say so; "I saved 2 hours" is still a good video.
- **Structure:**
  1. Cold open: the two totals side by side
  2. How I measured it, and why guessing doesn't count
  3. Task by task: which jobs shrank and which didn't
  4. The job where AI made things slower (there will be one)
  5. The exact weekly routine, step by step
  6. Who this will and won't work for
- **Verify:** tools and prices used; that the log is complete.

## Episode 5: LM Studio Tutorial for Beginners: No Coding, No Subscription

- **Keywords:** lm studio tutorial (about 28K searches a month, competition 38); lm studio (about 215K, competition 60)
- **Takeaway:** a no-terminal way to run AI privately. It pairs with episode 3 (Ollama) for people who don't want a command line.
- **Structure:**
  1. Cold open: chatting with a local model, with no terminal in sight
  2. What LM Studio is, and how it differs from Ollama in one sentence
  3. Download, install, and the first launch
  4. Find a model and check its size and licence before downloading
  5. The same three sole-trader tasks as episode 3, so viewers can compare
  6. Ollama vs LM Studio: which suits whom
- **Verify on recording day:** the current download page, install steps, interface labels, system requirements, and LM Studio's licence or terms for business use. Don't write any of these from memory.

## Episode 6: Doing Your Own Marketing? My AI System From Blank Page to Posted

- **Keyword:** ai tools for content creation (about 34K searches a month, competition 52)
- **Takeaway:** a repeatable weekly system that turns your real jobs into posts. It expands the existing Short "Stop starting from a blank page".
- **Structure:**
  1. Cold open: one finished job turned into a week of posts, on screen
  2. The input: three facts per job (problem, what you did, result)
  3. The prompt, with your past posts as voice examples
  4. Editing pass: remove anything invented (testimonials, numbers)
  5. Scheduling. Name the tool you actually use, and disclose any affiliate link.
  6. Link to the free 5-Minute AI Marketing Fix
- **Verify:** the scheduling tool's current free-plan limits; any platform rules for disclosing AI-written content.

## Episode 7: 5 Free AI Tools I'd Use If I Started a Business Today

- **Keyword:** free ai tools (about 186K searches a month, competition 46)
- **Takeaway:** five free tools, each matched to one start-up job (for example writing, design, admin, research, and private/local AI), each tested on a real task.
- **Rules:** only list tools you've used. Show each free plan's limits on screen with the date checked. Free tiers change often, so put the check date in the description.
- **Structure:** for each tool: the job, a 60-second demo, the free-plan limit, and the upgrade trap to watch for.
- **Tie-in:** AI Download Center as the place to compare alternatives. Say it's your own site.

## Episode 8: Can a Free Local AI Replace ChatGPT for Small Business Work?

- **Keyword:** local llm (about 69K searches a month, competition 48)
- **Takeaway:** a fair side-by-side test of one local model and one cloud assistant on 5 real business tasks, with clear scoring criteria.
- **The test:** the same prompts, the same tasks, scored on accuracy, edits needed, and time. Record model versions, hardware, settings and date. Call it a "field test", not a benchmark.
- **Structure:**
  1. Cold open: the scoreboard
  2. The rules and setup
  3. Tasks 1–5, shown side by side
  4. Where local AI won (privacy, cost, offline) and where cloud won (hard reasoning, speed)
  5. The verdict by type of user, not one overall winner
- **Verify:** the cloud tool's current plan and price, and its data-use settings.

## Episode 9: I Built an AI App for Sole Traders: What I Learned

- **Format:** an origin story, on camera. It expands your best-performing Short.
- **Takeaway:** what building SOLO AI taught you about where AI helps small businesses and where it doesn't.
- **Disclosure (required):** it's your own app. Say so in the first 30 seconds, in the description, and through YouTube's paid-promotion setting if anything about it is commercial.
- **Structure:**
  1. The problem you were trying to solve for yourself
  2. What you built, with a 60-second demo
  3. Three things you learned building it (only real experiences)
  4. What it can't do
  5. How to try it, and the tester call-out if testing is still open
- **Verify:** the app's current status (testing, Play Store, pricing); every claim about its features.

## Episode 10: AI Email Replies That Don't Sound Like a Robot

- **Keyword:** ai email writing (fewer than 750 searches a month, competition 28; a low-search but specific need)
- **Takeaway:** the three-input method (the message, your facts, your voice samples) and before-and-after examples.
- **Structure:**

  2. Why AI emails sound robotic: generic prompts produce generic text

  4. Five common emails: enquiry, quote follow-up, late payment, complaint, saying no
  5. A saved prompt template you can reuse
- **Note:** this expands Short 7 in `shorts-batch-01.md`.

## New Model, Real Test (reactive slot)

Keep one reactive video in reserve. When a new model or feature gets a big spike in searches (in vidIQ, keyword research → rising), make one test within days:

1. What changed, from the official release notes or announcement
2. Who can access it, and at what price
3. One small-business task, tested on screen
4. Verdict: is it worth switching?

On 15 Nov 2026, rising searches included Claude workflows ("claude animation skill", about 269K, competition 23) and "qwen 4" (about 366K, competition 46). These fade within weeks, so only chase one you can test on real business work.
