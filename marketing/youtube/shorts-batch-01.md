# Shorts batch 01

Eight Shorts, 30–50 seconds each: one finding, one caveat, one next step. Shorts 1–6 are cut from episodes 1–3, so record them on the same day and post them in the week after each long video. Shorts 7–8 stand alone.

**Rules for every Short**
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, with the words on screen as well as spoken.
- One idea per Short. No intro, no "hey guys".
- Captions on, with large text in the centre third of the screen.
- Pin a comment linking to the related long video. Clickable links in Short descriptions are unreliable, so use the related-video link field in YouTube Studio where you have it.
- Don't claim results you haven't measured. Lines marked `[NEEDS INFO]` come from your own tests.

### Short 4: "Turn one job into three posts"
**Hook:** "Never know what to post? Use the job you just finished."

> Here's the easiest marketing you'll do all week. Take one job you finished. Give AI three things: the problem, what you did, and the result. Ask for three short posts in your voice. Real work makes better posts than generic tips, and nobody can copy it. One warning: if AI adds a "happy customer" quote you never got, delete it. Testimonials have to be real.

**Title:** Turn one finished job into 3 social posts with AI #Shorts

**Hook:** "The job that quietly costs sole traders the most money."

Tell it which need a gentle nudge and which need a firmer message. It writes them in seconds. Then you hit send, because the hard part was never the wording. It was starting.

## Standalone

### Short 7: "Stop typing 'reply to this email'"
**Hook:** "If your AI emails sound like a robot, this is why."

> When you paste an email into AI and type "reply to this", it fills the gaps with guesses: prices, availability, policies. Give it three things instead: the email, your answer as bullet points, and one reply you've written before so it copies your voice. The AI writes the sentences and you supply the facts. Try it on your next enquiry.

**Title:** Why your AI emails sound like a robot (and the fix) #Shorts

### Short 8: "Don't paste this into AI"
**Hook:** "Before you paste client details into an AI tool, check this."

> Before you paste a client's address, contract or medical details into an AI chat, check two settings: whether your chats are used to train the model, and how long they're kept. Every tool is different and the settings change, so look them up for the tool you use. If you can't find a clear answer, remove the private details first, or use an AI that runs on your own laptop. I've got a free setup video for that.

**Title:** Check this before pasting client details into AI #Shorts
