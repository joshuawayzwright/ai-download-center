## Your photo for the thumbnails

- One photo per episode, shoulders up, face turned slightly towards the headline, a clear expression (curious, surprised or pointing at the text).
- Plain background, good light from a window in front of you. Phone camera is fine: use the back camera.
- Don't use AI-generated faces, and don't add company logos (OpenAI, Google, etc.).

## Recording setup (phone or webcam)

- **Camera:** eye level, landscape, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps. Lock exposure and focus on your face.
- **Sound matters more than picture:** a clip-on lavalier mic (about $20–40) or a quiet room with soft furnishings. Record a 10-second test and listen with headphones.
- **Screen capture:** 1920×1080, browser zoom 125% so text is readable on phones. Close notifications and blur client names.
- **Delivery:** read the script once, then talk it through in your own words. Stop and restart a sentence rather than fixing it later. Look at the lens, not the screen.
