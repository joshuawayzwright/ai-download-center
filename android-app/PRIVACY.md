# WrightWayz AI Download Centre — privacy notice

Effective: 25 October 2026
App ID: com.wrightwayz.aicentre

WrightWayz AI Centre is a public directory of AI tools and models. It is not a counselling, crisis-response, medical or clinical service.

## Information handled

- The AI catalogue is bundled in the app for offline browsing and local searches.
- When the app launches or a user taps Refresh, it requests a public JSON catalogue from GitHub over HTTPS. GitHub may process request data such as IP addresses under its own policies.
- Saved favourites are stored locally in Android app preferences, not transmitted to WrightWayz.
- Search terms filter the catalogue locally; the app does not send them to WrightWayz or AI providers.
- No login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, location permissions, notifications or in-app purchases are included in this release.
- Selecting an external link or sharing uses an Android browser/share sheet. Third parties operate under their own privacy policies.

## Removal

To delete saved favourites and cache, uninstall the app or clear its app data in Android Settings. This app has no WrightWayz account or personal-data backend.

## External sites

WrightWayz cannot independently guarantee the safety, quality, availability, licence or pricing of third-party listings. Review provider information before use. Some listings are sponsored and labelled as such.
