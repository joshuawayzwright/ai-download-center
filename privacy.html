Privacy

AI Download Center is designed as a public directory. Browsing the catalog does not require an account with us.

Information you provide

Free listing submissions may be provided through GitHub issue forms. Those submissions can be public. Do not include passwords, payment card details, private keys or other sensitive information in a GitHub issue.

Featured-listing checkout is hosted by Stripe. Stripe processes payment details and creates the subscription. AI Download Center does not receive or store your full card number.

Featured listings

At checkout, Stripe may collect your email address, tool name, tool website and information needed to process the subscription, billing and applicable tax. We use listing information to publish and maintain your sponsored placement and use contact information to provide customer support.

External services and links

The directory links to third-party AI providers, model registries, repositories and websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Typing filters the catalog locally. Selecting “Search Hugging Face online” sends the entered query to Hugging Face’s public model API; “Load more” requests additional results. Do not enter personal, sensitive or confidential information. Search text is not deliberately stored in this site’s URL or browser storage. Opening an old link containing a search term sends that URL to the hosting service before the page can remove the term from the address bar. Earlier history entries, shared links and hosting logs may still contain it.

Cookies and local browser data

The site stores a copy of public catalog metadata in localStorage under ai-download-center:catalog:v1 for fallback browsing. This is not a saved conversation or a search-history store. You can remove it with your browser’s site-data controls. The directory and Creator Studio use system fonts and a locally served emblem, rather than loading Google Fonts or Unsplash images. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process connection and request data; browser inspection cannot establish hosting-log retention. Stripe and other third-party destinations may set their own cookies when you use their services.

Creator Studio and external destinations

Creator Studio builds drafts in the open page without an AI service call. Reloading resets the generated draft. Copying or downloading a draft creates a separate copy under your control; clearing this website cannot remove clipboard, downloaded, shared or third-party copies. GitHub submissions may be public, and linked providers have separate terms.
