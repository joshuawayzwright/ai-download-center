    About the catalog

    A practical home for AI discovery.

    AI Download Center is a curated explorer for people who want to understand the landscape quickly. It brings together official model releases, practical assistants, self-hosted tools, image and video systems, and trusted community references in one organized catalog.

    The goal is simple: make it easier to compare options, find reliable sources, and learn what is available across the wider AI ecosystem without digging through dozens of scattered links and fragmented directories.

    Created and maintained by @joshuawayzwright.
