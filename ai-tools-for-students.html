How to choose

  • Stuck on a topic? Ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to explain it a different way.
  • Writing a research assignment? Use Perplexity to find sources, then read and cite the originals.
  • Presenting a project? Canva Magic Studio for slides and visuals.
  • Recording lectures? Whisper transcribes them for free.
