How to choose

  • Writing all day? Copilot and GrammarlyGO cover drafts and polish.
  • Posting on social media? Canva Magic Studio for images, HeyGen or Descript for video.
  • Chasing leads and customers? HubSpot AI is built around a CRM.
  • Doing the same admin every week? Automate it with Zapier AI or Relevance AI.
