How to choose

  • Need a voiceover without recording? Start with ElevenLabs or PlayHT.
  • Need captions or a transcript? Whisper is free and open source; Descript adds editing on top.
  • Need a video with a presenter but no camera? Look at Synthesia or HeyGen.
  • Need music for a video? Sonilo generates tracks matched to your footage.
  • Want to generate footage from a prompt? Try Runway or Pika.
