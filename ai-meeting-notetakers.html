AI meeting note takers: 5 tools that record and summarize your calls

Tired of taking notes in meetings? These 5 AI note takers from the AI Download Center catalog record your calls, write a transcript and summarize what was agreed. All five have a free plan, and each one links to its official site.

The main difference is how they record. Some send a bot that joins the call as a named participant; others record your computer’s audio so no bot appears. Several offer both. Always tell the people on the call that you are recording.

How to choose

  • Hate having a bot in the call? Granola records your computer audio; Otter, Fireflies.ai, Fathom and tl;dv also offer bot-free recording.
  • Run sales or customer calls? tl;dv sends notes into your CRM.
  • Want searchable transcripts of everything? Otter and Fireflies.ai focus on transcripts.
  • Prefer to keep audio on your own computer? Whisper transcribes recordings offline; see our offline AI tools guide.
