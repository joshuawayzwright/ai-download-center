Free AI tools starter kit: 24 tools you can use without paying

New to AI tools, or don’t want to pay for one yet? This kit picks 24 tools from the AI Download Center catalog that are free to use or have a free plan, and groups them by the job you want done: chat and research, running AI on your own computer, coding, images, audio and video, and everyday work. Every entry links to the maker’s official site.

Of the 24, 6 are open source, 1 is free, and 17 have a free plan with paid upgrades. Free plans have limits that change often, so check each tool’s pricing page. This list isn’t ranked, and we haven’t tested or benchmarked these tools. Not sure whether you want a free plan, a local app or open source? Read what “free” really means in AI tools.

Where to start

  • Never used an AI assistant? Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in your browser and ask it to help with something you’re working on today.
  • Need answers with sources? Try Perplexity.
  • Care about privacy, or want to avoid subscriptions? Install LM Studio, Jan or GPT4All and run a model on your own computer. See our local LLM apps guide.
  • Write code? Try GitHub Copilot or Cursor in your editor, or Aider in the terminal. See our AI coding assistants guide.
  • Make graphics? Try Ideogram for images with text, or Canva Magic Studio for finished designs. See our AI image generators guide.
